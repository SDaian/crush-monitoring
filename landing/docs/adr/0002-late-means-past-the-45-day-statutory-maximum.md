# "Late" means past the 45-day statutory maximum

"Filed N days late" — the page's signature accusation — is computed as
filing lag beyond the STOCK Act's 45-day outer deadline
(`daysLate = max(0, (filing_date − tx_date) − 45)`), not the 30-day
awareness rule. Awareness dates aren't in public data, so a 30-day basis
(39% of filings "late") is contestable row-by-row, while the 45-day basis
(15%) is an indisputable legal violation; on a credibility-branded page the
accusation must be unfalsifiable. Revisiting toward the stricter basis
later is an accepted possibility — this records why 45 was the launch
choice.
